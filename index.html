Good to know

How do I book?

Pick your room and dates above, then tap Request booking. Your request is copied and Messenger opens, so you can paste it and send. We confirm your slot there.

How does the down payment work?

A 50% GCash down payment locks your dates. The balance is paid on arrival.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 12:00 NN. Early check-in and late check-out depend on availability.

Can we bring extra guests?

Each room includes 2 guests. Additional guests are charged per night, up to the room's maximum.

What if the weather turns bad?

Talk to us on Messenger. We can usually move your dates for a storm warning.
